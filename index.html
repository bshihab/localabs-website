Frequently asked

Common questions.

Does Localabs replace my doctor?
No. Localabs translates lab reports into language you can understand and helps you prepare for the conversation with your clinician. Every report ends with explicit questions to bring up at your next visit.
What lab reports does it work with?
Any printed lab report with test names, values, and reference ranges — CBC, comprehensive metabolic, lipid, thyroid, A1c, vitamin D, and most other standard panels. Multi-page reports are supported via the document scanner.
Why on-device?
Lab data is among the most sensitive personal data you have. Running the AI on your phone means no third party — not even us — ever sees your results.
What model does it use?
Google's MedGemma 4B — a medical-tuned Gemma — quantized to ~2.5 GB and downloaded once. The model runs on your iPhone's GPU via llama.cpp on Metal.
How much storage does it use?
The default model is ~2.5 GB, downloaded once. Scanned reports add a few hundred KB each. Everything else (chat state, settings, profile) is tiny.
What iPhones does it run on?
iPhone 15 Pro and later — same hardware floor as Apple Intelligence. A 4-billion-parameter model needs 8 GB of RAM to comfortably fit alongside iOS and the KV cache, which excludes pre-A17-Pro devices. iPad isn't supported in this release.
Does it need an Apple Watch?
No. Phone-only users get steps, walking distance, walking speed, and manually-logged vitals. A paired Watch adds resting HR, HRV, VO₂ max, sleep, and other passive measurements.
Will my profile ever leave my phone?
No. Age, conditions, medications and family history live in your device's secure storage and are read locally each time a chat or analysis runs.
